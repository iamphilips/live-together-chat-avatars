Live Together Chat Avatars
Emotion-inspired avatars for Live Together Chat, created to support connection, self-expression, warmth, and belonging.

About Live Together Chat
Live Together Chat is a small shared space for people who happen to be online at the same time.

The experience is designed around a simple idea: a conversation does not need to be perfect to feel meaningful. A short message, a kind reply, or simply knowing that someone else is present can make a digital space feel a little more human.

The chat interface gently guides people through a few steps before they join:

Read the community and safety reminders.

Choose a temporary display name.

Choose or upload an avatar.

Confirm and enter the conversation.

The goal is to make joining feel clear, lightweight, and emotionally safe.

Why emotion-inspired avatars?
The default avatars are designed around emotions, moods, and small personality cues rather than fixed gender roles.

They are intentionally playful, simple, and expressive. Users can choose a character that feels close to their mood in that moment, without needing to explain themselves.

From the perspective of positive psychology, these avatars are meant to support:

Self-expression — giving people an easy way to show a little of how they feel.

Belonging — helping each participant feel like a visible part of the shared space.

Warmth — making the interface feel friendlier and less clinical.

Autonomy — letting users choose how they want to appear.

Positive connection — creating small visual cues that can make conversation easier to begin.

The avatars are not personality tests and do not label users. They are simply friendly starting points.

Character Collection
The collection currently includes emotion-inspired characters such as:

Whimsy · Cheery · Mellow · Dreamy · Giddy · Wonder · Spunky · Gloomy · Hope · Affection · Content · Bravery · Playful · Curious · Tender · Bold · Easygoing · Thoughtful · Delight · Silly · Alert · Calm · Cozy · Sunny

Each avatar can be used as a default randomized profile image or selected manually by the user.

Design Direction
The avatar set follows a simple visual language:

flat illustration

minimal colors

clear facial expressions

small-size readability

unisex character design

warm, rural-inspired charm

transparent webp assets

enough visual spacing for easy cropping and reuse

The intention is to keep the characters friendly and recognizable even when displayed as small chat avatars.

Safety and Community Care
Live Together Chat includes visible reminders before a user enters the conversation.

The space is designed for friendly conversation and is not a medical or emergency service. Users are encouraged to seek appropriate professional or emergency help when needed and to avoid sharing sensitive personal information.

These reminders are part of the experience because emotional safety matters just as much as visual warmth.

File Naming
Use lowercase English filenames without spaces.

Examples:

wonder.webp
easygoing.webp
thoughtful.webp
sunny.webp
This keeps the files easy to reference from JavaScript, CSS, or other web components.

A small design principle
People do not always need a big conversation. Sometimes they only need a small sign that they are not alone in the room.

Live Together Chat uses these avatars as one of those small signs.
